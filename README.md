## 📌 Executive Summary

I am an **Electronics Engineer** specializing in the intersection of **hardware architectures, real-time embedded systems, and on-device machine learning (Edge AI / TinyML)**. My core engineering focus spans:

* ⚡ **Embedded Systems & Firmware Development**: Designing deterministic bare-metal and RTOS firmware for ARM Cortex-M and ESP32 microcontrollers, managing standard bus protocols (I2C, SPI, UART, CAN).
* 🎛️ **Digital System Design & FPGA/RTL**: Authoring modular, synthesizable Verilog HDL architectures, parameterizable clock dividers, and finite state machines (FSMs) verified through ModelSim/QuestaSim.
* 🔋 **Control Systems & Power Electronics**: Engineering adaptive, safety-critical control algorithms (Parallel Online Recursive Least Squares, Constrained Model Predictive Control) for multi-cell Lithium-ion Battery Management Systems (BMS).
* 🧠 **Edge AI & TinyML**: Quantizing, optimizing, and deploying deep learning models (e.g., autoencoders and convolutional neural networks) directly onto memory-constrained microcontrollers for real-time anomaly detection and edge inferencing.
